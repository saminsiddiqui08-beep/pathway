Start over?

For every country on this phone, starting over removes:

  • Your answers to the questions
  • The steps you marked done on the Plan tab
  • Your own dates

It keeps the ticks in roadmap checklists, My list on the Countries tab, the ticks in Toolkit guides, what you entered in the Toolkit's tools, and your reminder choices, so a new plan reminds you the same way. It cannot be undone.
